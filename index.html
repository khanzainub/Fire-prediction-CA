Model equations

Cellular automaton on a square raster (30 m for Landsat, 10 m for Sentinel-2). Cell states: unburnt, burning, burnt, blocked (outside area, water, built-up, bare, snow). Every step of Δt minutes, each burning cell tries to ignite its 8 neighbours j (distance d, ×√2 on diagonals):


p = 1 - exp(-R · Δt / d)
R = R0 · F_j · M_j · f_RH · f_T · f_rain · f_wind · f_slope   (m/min)

Effective fuel   Fn = clamp((NDVI_green - NDVI_dry) / 0.5, 0, 1)
                 F  = 0.25 + 0.75·Fn
Moisture         m_v = clamp((NDMI + 0.2) / 0.6, 0, 1)
                 m_s = clamp(theta_soil / 0.4, 0, 1)
                 M   = exp(-2·(0.7·m_v + 0.3·m_s))
Humidity         f_RH = 1 - 0.9·(RH/100)^2
Temperature      f_T  = exp(0.03·(T - 25))
Rain             API  = sum P(t-h)·exp(-h/48), h = 0..72 h
                 f_rain = exp(-0.3·API)
Wind             f_wind = exp(c1·V)·exp(c2·V·(cos(th) - 1))
                 c1 = 0.045, c2 = 0.131
Slope            f_slope = exp(0.078·slope_deg), limited to +-45 deg


F, M and slope are fixed per cell (from satellite and SRTM at native resolution). Weather comes from a lattice of Open-Meteo points and is interpolated to every cell each step: wind as u/v vectors (bilinear), temperature reduced to sea level, interpolated and lowered again with the 30 m DEM (6.5 K/km), humidity from interpolated vapour pressure and the cell temperature, rain index bilinear. Time is interpolated linearly between hourly values. Because p uses R·Δt/d, results do not depend on the step length as long as R·Δt < d. The wind and slope terms follow Alexandridis et al. (2008). R0 and the other constants are starting values and need calibration for your region.


Validation: predicted burnt cells are compared with FIRMS detections (each dilated to its pixel footprint) and with cells where dNBR (or RBR) exceeds the threshold. Precision, recall, F1 and IoU are reported, and the map layer "Model vs reference" shows agreement.
